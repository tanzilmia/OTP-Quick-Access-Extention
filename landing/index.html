Privacy first

Your email stays your email

The extension is designed so your inbox content never leaves your device.

  • Read-only access. Only the gmail.readonly scope is requested — it can't send, delete, or modify mail.
  • On-device processing. Messages are fetched from Google and parsed inside the popup. Code extraction happens locally.
  • No servers, no tracking. There is no backend. Your email content is never uploaded to us or any third party.
  • You're in control. Sign out anytime to revoke access and clear the cached session from your browser.
Limited Use disclosure. OTP Quick Access's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely to display your one-time codes in the popup and is never transferred elsewhere, used for advertising, or read by humans.

Transparency

Every permission, explained

Here's exactly what the extension asks for and why it needs it.

PermissionWhy it's needed
gmail.readonly Search recent messages and read their content to find one-time verification codes. Read access only — no sending or editing.
identity Lets you sign in to Google through Chrome's secure OAuth flow so the extension can obtain a read-only access token.
storage Remembers your sign-out state so the session doesn't silently restore after you choose to disconnect.
googleapis.com host access Allows the popup to talk directly to Google's Gmail API over HTTPS. No other websites are accessed.
FAQ

Good questions

Does this extension store or send my emails anywhere?

No. There is no server behind the extension. Messages are requested from Google's Gmail API and parsed inside the popup on your own device. Your email content is never uploaded to us or shared with any third party.

Can it send email or change my account?

No. It requests only the read-only Gmail scope, which technically cannot send, delete, label, or modify anything in your mailbox.

How far back does it look for codes?

It searches messages from roughly the last 7 days that match verification-style keywords, then shows the most recent codes it can confidently identify.

Why didn't a code show up?

Some emails use unusual formatting, or the message may not match the search keywords. You can still open Gmail as usual. The extension also deliberately ignores look-alike numbers such as years, ZIP codes, and order numbers to avoid showing the wrong value.

How do I disconnect?

Click the sign-out icon in the popup header. This revokes the access token and clears the cached session from your browser. You can reconnect anytime, or use Switch account to connect a different Google account.

Does it update automatically while closed?

No. The list refreshes when you open the popup or tap Refresh. It does not poll your inbox in the background.
