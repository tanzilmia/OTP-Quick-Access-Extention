Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OTP Quick Access Chrome extension ("the extension", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By installing and using the extension, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Single purpose

OTP Quick Access has one purpose: to read recent messages in your Gmail inbox, identify one-time passcodes (OTP / verification codes), and display them in the extension's popup so you can copy them quickly. It does nothing else.

2. Information the extension accesses

  • Gmail message content (read-only). With your permission, the extension uses the Gmail API with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly scope to search for and read recent verification-style emails and extract the numeric codes they contain. It cannot send, delete, or modify any mail.
  • Google OAuth access token. Chrome's identity service issues a short-lived access token after you sign in. The extension uses it only to call the Gmail API on your behalf. The token is managed by Chrome and is not transmitted to us.
  • Local preferences. Your theme choice (light or dark) is saved in your browser's local storage, and a flag recording whether you have signed out is saved in chrome.storage.local. Neither contains email content.

3. How information is used

Gmail data is used exclusively, and in real time, to locate and display one-time codes within the extension popup on your device. Email content is held in memory only while the popup is open and is not written to disk by the extension.

4. Data storage and transmission

  • No backend servers. The extension has no server of its own. All processing happens locally in your browser.
  • No collection or sharing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your email content or any personal data. The only network requests the extension makes are directly to Google's servers (googleapis.com and oauth2.googleapis.com) to authenticate and to fetch your messages.
  • No analytics or tracking. The extension contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

5. Limited Use disclosure

OTP Quick Access's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Gmail data is used only to provide the user-facing feature of displaying one-time codes.
  • Gmail data is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide or improve that feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with appropriate consent.
  • Gmail data is not used for serving advertisements.
  • No humans read your Gmail data, except where you give affirmative consent for specific messages, as necessary for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse), or to comply with applicable law.

6. Your choices and control

  • Sign out. Use the sign-out control in the popup to revoke the access token and clear the cached session from your browser at any time.
  • Switch account. You can disconnect the current account and connect a different Google account.
  • Revoke access with Google. You can remove the extension's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Uninstall. Removing the extension deletes its locally stored preferences from your browser.

7. Data retention

The extension does not retain your email content. Codes and message data exist only in memory while the popup is open and are discarded when it closes. Locally stored preferences remain until you sign out, clear your browser data, or uninstall the extension.

8. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through the Chrome Web Store listing.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the extension's data practices, contact us at support@example.com.
